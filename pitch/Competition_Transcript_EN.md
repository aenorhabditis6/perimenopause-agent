# Market, competitors and target positioning: speaker script

About 60–75 seconds.

We looked at five similar products. anna is the closest: it already uses existing wearable data to give perimenopausal users a small daily action. Harmoni focuses more on AI chat and symptom logging; Peri monitors passively with its own sensor; Oura offers its own data plus a symptom assessment; and Play Health connects lifestyle logging with the doctor's side.

These products each have a head start, and we still need to catch up on real data integration, advice quality and user experience.

So we narrow our target user further: perimenopausal women who already have a wearable, live busy lives, and don't want to log every day. We want to combine shopping receipts and real plans to put advice inside things she is already doing. This combination of context is the differentiation we want to test; for now we show the experience with synthetic data.
