# Product Features | Speaker script

About 75–90 seconds. The slide shows only three short bullets; the rest is said out loud.

## 1. All women, ages 40–55

Our initial target users are all women aged 40 to 55; she doesn't need clear symptoms or a diagnosis first. The menopause transition is a natural life stage most women go through, but when it starts, how long it lasts and how it feels differ from person to person. We want support to be there before she needs it. This age range is who we serve, not a diagnostic criterion.

## 2. No tedious input

We've all been there: if you want an agent to really know you, you have to keep telling it your background, how you feel each day and the details of your life. Providing accurate data takes a lot of effort in itself. We don't want health support to become one more daily chore. So, with the user's permission, we want to use the information already in her wearable, shopping receipts and calendar, so the agent keeps up in the background, without her logging every day or starting every question. Only when key information that would change the advice is missing does it ask one short question.

## 3. Agent suggests. You decide.

The agent only makes suggestions; the final decision is always hers. For example, if it notices her sleep has changed recently and she has tennis with friends tonight, it can send a heads-up explaining what it noticed, why it's mentioning it, and what options she has. It won't stop her from playing tennis, and it won't change or cancel her plans on its own. She can take the advice, ignore it, or just go and enjoy the time with her friends. Our goal is to raise awareness and help her judge, not to make decisions for her.

---

Note: this is a proposed experience; this demo uses synthetic data only, and real integrations and effects are still to be validated. The target age range is not a judgment about anyone's perimenopause status.
